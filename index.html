Sustainable Energy Consulting is an engineering company specializing in renewables and energy efficiency, and expert in premium quality thermal, electrical and water systems. SEC is a one-stop complete energy solutions, providing consulting services from energy audit and MEP design to project management till commissioning, for industrial, commercial and residential markets.

At SEC:

  1. We guide customers from MEP and system's design till project completion, through a choice of optimized solutions for any situation.
  2. We work in close partnership with world-class OEMs, architects, project engineers, contractors and local suppliers.

  4. Clients are backed by extensive component and service warranty.
